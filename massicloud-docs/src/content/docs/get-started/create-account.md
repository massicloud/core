<Steps>

1. **Go to [app.massicloud.dz](https://app.massicloud.dz)**

   This is the MassiCloud portal — the web interface where you manage your projects.

2. **Click "Sign up"**

   Enter your email and a password. There's no credit card required; the Hobby tier is free forever.

3. **Verify your email**

   Click the link in the email we send you. (If you don't receive it within a minute, check your spam folder.)

4. **You're in**

   You'll land on your dashboard — empty for now. Next, we'll create a project.

</Steps>

## A note on regions

MassiCloud only operates in Algerian datacenters. There's no region selector because there's only one region: Algeria. Your data will physically reside in Algiers.
